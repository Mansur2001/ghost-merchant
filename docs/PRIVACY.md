GuriKaabe is a delivery coordination service operated by **[YOUR LEGAL NAME OR COMPANY]**
("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do not do.

## The short version

We collect the minimum needed to bring an order to your door: your phone number, where to
deliver, what you asked for, and any photos you choose to attach. We do not sell your data,
we do not share it with advertisers, and we never see or hold your money.

## What we collect

| Data | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| **Phone number** | It is your account. We send a one-time code to confirm you hold it, and the driver uses it to reach you at the door. | While your account exists |
| **Delivery location** (GPS, optional) and **landmark** (required) | So a driver can find you. Addresses are unreliable in our service area; the landmark is the primary instruction and the GPS point is an aid. | With the order |
| **Order contents and chat messages** | To fulfil the order and resolve disputes. | With the order |
| **Photos you attach** | A reference photo helps the driver find the right item; a delivery-proof photo protects both you and the driver in a dispute. | With the order |
| **Payment receipt details** (transaction reference, sender number or name, amount) | To match your transfer to your order. | With the order |
| **Photo of an ID** — *only if you apply to be a driver or operator* | Drivers carry other people's money and goods, so we confirm who they are before issuing an account. | **Deleted the moment the application is decided.** Never collected from customers. |

We do **not** collect: your contacts, your location in the background or when the app is
closed, your browsing or app activity, advertising identifiers, or any biometric data.

**Customers are never asked for an ID.** That only applies to people applying to work as a
driver or operator, and their document is destroyed as soon as the application is approved or
declined — we keep only the fact that it was checked, and who checked it.

## Money

**We never take custody of your money.** Payment goes directly from you to the merchant's
mobile-money account using your own phone's dialer. We do not process the payment, hold a
balance, or store card or bank details — we only read the confirmation message the telecom
sends, to know your order is paid. Refunds are arranged directly and off-platform.

## How your data is used and shared

Your order is visible to:
- **you**, on the number you verified;
- **the driver assigned to your order**, and only while assigned — a driver cannot see orders
  that aren't theirs;
- **our operators**, who coordinate deliveries and resolve payment problems.

We share data with **no one else**. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do
not use it for advertising or profiling. We may disclose information if legally compelled, or
where necessary to prevent fraud or harm.

## Where your data lives

On servers we operate ourselves at **[HOSTING PROVIDER AND COUNTRY]**. The service does not
use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to an order is checked on every
request against the phone number you verified. Login codes and staff passwords are stored
only as salted hashes — never in a readable form. No system is perfectly secure, but we do
not collect data we don't need, which is the most effective protection available.

## Your choices and rights

- **Location and camera are optional** and asked for only at the moment they're used. You can
  decline and still order using a landmark; you can decline the camera and order without a photo.
- **Access or deletion:** contact us at **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]** and we will provide a copy of
  your data or delete your account. We may retain payment and delivery records where required
  for accounting, dispute resolution or legal compliance.
- **Withdrawing consent:** stop using the app and request deletion.

## Retention

Order records, including chat and photos, are kept while your account is active and for a
period afterwards for dispute resolution and accounting — **[STATE YOUR RETENTION PERIOD,
e.g. 24 months]**. Expired login codes are deleted automatically.

GuriKaabe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
