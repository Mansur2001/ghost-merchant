GuriKaabe is a delivery coordination service operated by **OPENARMS ALLIANCE LLC**
("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do not do.

## The short version

We collect the minimum needed to bring an order to your door: your phone number, where to
deliver, what you asked for, and any photos you choose to attach. We do not sell your data,
we do not share it with advertisers, and we never see or hold your money.

## What we collect

| Data | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| **Email address** (optional) | Only if you sign in with email, or add one to get your codes there. We send a one-time code to confirm it is yours. | Until you remove it or your account is deleted |
| **Phone number** | To sign in by text (we send a one-time code to confirm you hold it), and on each order so the driver can reach you at the door. | While your account exists |
| **Delivery location** (GPS, optional) and **landmark** (required) | So a driver can find you. Addresses are unreliable in our service area; the landmark is the primary instruction and the GPS point is an aid. | With the order |
| **Order contents and chat messages** | To fulfil the order and resolve disputes. | With the order |
| **Photos you attach** | A reference photo helps the driver find the right item; a delivery-proof photo protects both you and the driver in a dispute. | With the order |
| **Payment details** (amount, and the transaction number from your mobile-money message, and which wallet you used, if you or our operator enter it) | So our operator can confirm your payment arrived, and so one payment can't be counted for two orders. | With the order |
| **A rating** (1–5 stars, optional comment) after a finished job | So riders can choose good drivers and we can spot bad service. Riders see only a driver's average. | While your account exists |
| **Item suggestions** (a name, a note, a photo you take) | So we can add things you want to the shop. A suggestion we reject loses its photo at once; one we accept becomes a public shop picture. | Until decided (rejected photos), or as long as the item is sold |
| **Your wallet** (balance, top-ups, payments, refunds, withdrawals) | GuriKaabe is licensed to hold a customer balance. Every movement is a ledger line, kept to settle any question about your money. | While your account exists, then as accounting law requires |
| **A first name you choose** (optional) | To greet you in the app. | Until you change or remove it |
| **Name and phone number**, and an email if the admin uses one — *drivers and operators only* | To create your staff account. We send you a one-time setup code, by text or email, so you can choose your own PIN or password. | While your account exists |
| **A driver's photo, plate and vehicle** — *drivers only* | Riders choose a driver, and recognise them at the kerb, by photo, first name, plate and rating. Shown only to signed-in customers. | While the driver works with us |
| **Photos of an ID, front and back** — *only if you apply to be a driver or operator* | Drivers carry other people's money and goods, so we confirm who they are before issuing an account. | **Both images are deleted the moment the application is decided.** Never collected from customers. |

We do **not** collect: your contacts, your location in the background or when the app is
closed, your browsing or app activity, advertising identifiers, or any biometric data.

**Customers are never asked for an ID.** That only applies to people applying to work as a
driver or operator, and both images are destroyed as soon as the application is approved or
declined — we keep only the fact that it was checked, and who checked it. We do not keep a
copy after the decision, so a future breach cannot expose the ID of someone who applied.

## Money

You pay GuriKaabe's merchant mobile-money account with your own phone's dialer (or approve a
prompt on your phone), or from your **GuriKaabe Wallet**. We never store card or bank details or
your mobile-money PIN. Our operator checks that a payment reached the merchant before an order
— or a wallet top-up — counts as paid, using the transaction number from the telecom, which can
only ever be used once. Money in your wallet is yours: it is spent only on orders you place,
refunds of wallet-paid orders go back to it at once, and you can ask to withdraw it to your
own number.

## How your data is used and shared

Your order is visible to:
- **you**, on the number you verified;
- **the driver assigned to your order**, and only while assigned — a driver cannot see orders
  that aren't theirs;
- **our operators**, who coordinate deliveries and resolve payment problems.

**Text messages and email.** Login and setup codes are delivered by **Twilio**, by SMS or,
if you chose it, by email through Twilio SendGrid. It receives your phone number or email and
the text of the message (which contains the code) only to deliver it, and nothing else about
you or your orders.

**Riders and drivers.** When you book a ride you see a few nearby drivers' first names,
photos, plates, ratings and how many minutes away they are — never where they are, and never
their phone number. A driver sees your pickup only after accepting.

**Help chat.** Questions you type in the help chat that our built-in answers can't cover are
sent to a hosted AI model (Groq) to word an answer, with the status of the order you are asking
about — never your name, phone, address or delivery code. Nothing you type there is stored by
us. The assistant can suggest a cart or a ride for you to check; it cannot place, pay for or
change anything.

**Maps.** Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License
(openstreetmap.org/copyright). The street map is drawn with Protomaps from a copy kept on our
own servers. When you search for a place, the words you typed are sent through our server to
OpenStreetMap's search service (Nominatim) — nothing about who searched.

Apart from that, we share data with **no one else**. We do not sell or rent personal
information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We may disclose information if legally compelled, or
where necessary to prevent fraud or harm.

## Where your data lives

On servers we operate ourselves at **Vultr, in Frankfurt, Germany**, including your photos,
which are stored on the same servers rather than with a cloud photo service. Text messages
and emails pass through Twilio, which may process them outside your country. The service does not use
third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to an order is checked on every
request against the phone number you verified. Login codes, setup codes, driver PINs and
staff passwords are stored only as salted hashes — never in a readable form. Staff choose
their own PIN or password; nobody at GuriKaabe ever knows it. No system is perfectly secure, but we do
not collect data we don't need, which is the most effective protection available.

## Your choices and rights

- **Location and camera are optional** and asked for only at the moment they're used. You can
  decline and still order using a landmark; you can decline the camera and order without a photo.
- **Access or deletion:** contact us at **gurikaabe@gmail.com** and we will provide a copy of
  your data or delete your account. We may retain payment and delivery records where required
  for accounting, dispute resolution or legal compliance.
- **Withdrawing consent:** stop using the app and request deletion.

## Retention

Order records, including chat and photos, are kept while your account is active and for a
period afterwards for dispute resolution and accounting — **24 months**. Login codes are deleted once used or after 5 minutes; staff setup codes once
used or after 24 hours.

## Children

GuriKaabe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
