# 2026-11-11 — The full app from the stakeholder mockup

The owner brought the stakeholders' one-page design (home with eight services, choose your
bajaj driver, a grocery cart with free delivery, five-step tracking, a wallet, AI Caawiye with
quick chips) and asked for the app to be completed to it. Branch `full-app`.

## Decided with the owner (questions asked before building)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| The mockup lets the rider pick a driver at different prices | **Customer picks, one fare.** The chosen driver is offered the paid ride for **30 s**; decline or silence → the **nearest free driver** automatically. |
| Wallet as a main tab, top-ups, pay with wallet | **The owner holds the licence: the wallet is ON.** Update CLAUDE.md. |
| "Raashin iiga dalbo" / "Bajaj ii dalbo" | **Drafts, customer confirms.** The AI fills a cart or a ride; it never orders or pays. |
| Farmashiye, Farsamo, Biyo, Delivery | **All of them, now.** Listings as picture cards with licence-free stock photos; customers may suggest items with a photo. |
| Who approves suggestions | **Operator reviews, admin prices and publishes.** |
| Farsamo model | **Prepaid visit fee by trade**; technicians are a new staff role; extra work is a new prepaid order quoted in the app. |
| "1–2 daqiiqo" and "FREE DELIVERY" | **Live ETA + an admin promo.** No fixed-minutes promise anywhere. |

## What was built

- **Choose your driver** (`domain/offers.js`, `commands/dispatch.js`): the ride quote lists up
  to 5 free drivers per vehicle (first name, photo, plate, rating, minutes away — no phone, no
  position). Paid → offered (PAID_UNASSIGNED + `offer_driver_id/offer_expires_at`); accept →
  DISPATCHED; decline / 30 s sweep → `released_by` and automatic dispatch. Drivers see offers at
  the top of their list with a countdown and a buzz. Anyone with a live offer is skipped by
  every other dispatch.
- **Parcels** (`PARCEL`): sender's door → receiver's door, fee by distance, contents + receiver's
  phone, delivery proof rules (photo at collection checked against the sender's pin).
- **Farsamo** (`SERVICE`, `trades`): visit fee per trade, technicians (`drivers.role`,
  `trades`), photo to start, the customer's code to finish, extra work as a prepaid child order.
- **Ratings**, driver **plate and photo** (admin), public pictures checked by their bytes.
- **Catalog**: 13 categories across the tiles, 77 items, 70 with stock photos
  (`frontend/shared/img/catalog/LICENSES.txt`: 62 CC0/public domain, 8 CC BY/BY-SA credited).
  Pharmacy is over-the-counter only. Admin picks a stock photo or uploads one. A one-time
  additive upgrade brings an existing install (production) the new categories, pictures, cities
  and trades without touching its prices.
- **Customer suggestions** with a photo → operator review → admin price & publish.
- **Free delivery** switch (admin, audited, minimum order), shown as banners.
- **Wallet ON**; USSD top-ups the customer claims and an operator confirms with the merchant
  phone's reference (UNIQUE like any payment), until WaafiPay has keys. Rides can be paid from
  the wallet in one tap.
- **Inbox** (the bell): derived, only "read up to" stored.
- **AI drafts** (`domain/assistantDraft.js`): requests become cart / ride / nearby drafts;
  questions still go to the FAQ. The chips from the mockup sit in the chat panel.
- **Customer app** redesigned to the mockup: five tabs, home (city, bell, greeting, banner,
  search, eight tiles, ETA strip, AI card), picture-card shops, choose-driver list and payment
  row, five-step tracker with ETA and driver card, rating, share, wallet tab, profile.
  `features.js` holds the new screens. Light theme by default, brand yellow.
- **Cities**: Mogadishu, Boosaaso, Hargeisa, Garowe, Galkayo, Kismayo, Baidoa, Beledweyne —
  the picker only centres maps; each city got a market (approximate pins).
- **Operator**: Suggestions, Top-ups, Promo panels (`extras.js`); driver role/trades/plate/photo
  and ratings; trades and pictures in the catalog.
