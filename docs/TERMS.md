## What GuriKaabe does

GuriKaabe coordinates deliveries: you tell us what you need and where you are, you pay the
merchant from your own mobile money, and a driver brings the order to you. We arrange the
delivery. **We never hold your money** — payment goes straight from your phone to the
merchant's mobile-money number.

## Delivery

- Delivery times are estimates. Roads, weather, security and the network can delay an order.
- The driver may take a photo at delivery as proof for both of you.

- Because we never hold your money, refunds are sent by our staff from the merchant's phone,
  not processed automatically by the app.
