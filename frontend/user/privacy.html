GuriKaabe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

GuriKaabe is a delivery coordination service operated by OPENARMS ALLIANCE LLC ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do not do.

The short version

We collect the minimum needed to bring an order to your door: your phone number, where to deliver, what you asked for, and any photos you choose to attach. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we never see or hold your money.

We do not collect: your contacts, your location in the background or when the app is closed, your browsing or app activity, advertising identifiers, or any biometric data.

Customers are never asked for an ID. That only applies to people applying to work as a driver or operator, and both images are destroyed as soon as the application is approved or declined — we keep only the fact that it was checked, and who checked it. We do not keep a copy after the decision, so a future breach cannot expose the ID of someone who applied.

Money

We never take custody of your money. Payment goes directly from you to the merchant's mobile-money account using your own phone's dialer. We do not process the payment, hold a balance, or store card or bank details. Our operator checks that your payment reached the merchant, then marks your order paid. Refunds are arranged directly and off-platform.

How your data is used and shared

Your order is visible to:

  • you, on the number you verified;
  • the driver assigned to your order, and only while assigned — a driver cannot see orders that aren't theirs;
  • our operators, who coordinate deliveries and resolve payment problems.

Text messages and email. Login and setup codes are delivered by Twilio, by SMS or, if you chose it, by email through Twilio SendGrid. It receives your phone number or email and the text of the message (which contains the code) only to deliver it, and nothing else about you or your orders.

Apart from that, we share data with no one else. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We may disclose information if legally compelled, or where necessary to prevent fraud or harm.

Where your data lives

On servers we operate ourselves at Vultr, in Frankfurt, Germany, including your photos, which are stored on the same servers rather than with a cloud photo service. Text messages and emails pass through Twilio, which may process them outside your country. The service does not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to an order is checked on every request against the phone number you verified. Login codes, setup codes, driver PINs and staff passwords are stored only as salted hashes — never in a readable form. Staff choose their own PIN or password; nobody at GuriKaabe ever knows it. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not collect data we don't need, which is the most effective protection available.

Your choices and rights

  • Location and camera are optional and asked for only at the moment they're used. You can decline and still order using a landmark; you can decline the camera and order without a photo.
  • Access or deletion: contact us at gurikaabe@gmail.com and we will provide a copy of your data or delete your account. We may retain payment and delivery records where required for accounting, dispute resolution or legal compliance.
  • Withdrawing consent: stop using the app and request deletion.

Retention

Order records, including chat and photos, are kept while your account is active and for a period afterwards for dispute resolution and accounting — 24 months. Login codes are deleted once used or after 5 minutes; staff setup codes once used or after 24 hours.

Children

GuriKaabe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
