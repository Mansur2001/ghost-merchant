What GuriKaabe does

GuriKaabe coordinates deliveries: you tell us what you need and where you are, you pay the merchant from your own mobile money, and a driver brings the order to you. We arrange the delivery. We never hold your money — payment goes straight from your phone to the merchant's mobile-money number.
