    FAQ

    What is Ghost Merchant?

    A local-first way to order goods from the market and have a driver bring them to you. You pay the driver directly from your own phone — no bank account or foreign app needed.

    How do I pay?

    After you create an order, tap Pay now. Your phone's dialer opens with the EVC Plus / eDahab code pre-filled. Approve the transfer and this page updates automatically once payment is confirmed.

    Is my phone number safe?

    Your number is your identity here — it isn't shared with advertisers or foreign servers. Everything runs on self-hosted infrastructure.

    Why do you need a landmark?

    Street addresses are unreliable, so drivers navigate by a nearby landmark (e.g. "green house next to the yellow pharmacy"). A reference photo helps too.

    How do I track my order?

    The progress timeline and chat update live as the driver accepts, shops, and delivers. You'll also see a delivery-proof photo when it arrives.
